Implement Phase 1: Smart AI WhatsApp Qualification System for Kinglike Luxury CRM.

Important:
This is Phase 1 only.
Do NOT connect WhatsApp API yet.
Do NOT send any real WhatsApp messages.
Do NOT modify payments, authentication, public property pages, existing Meta Pull Sync logic, webhook logic, or unrelated AI features.
Build the internal CRM structure, admin UI, permissions, AI prompt, conversation storage, and agent visibility rules only.

Goal:
When a new Meta lead enters the CRM, create an internal AI WhatsApp qualification workflow.
The AI should behave like a professional Kinglike Luxury real estate advisor, prepare a natural WhatsApp-style conversation, collect client information gradually, and generate reports for the assigned agent.

Core AI behavior:
The AI must not act like a dumb form.
It should:
- Speak in professional Arabic by default.
- Use the client name if available.
- Be polite, patient, calm, and respectful.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Keep messages short and natural.
- Keep the client interested without pressure.
- Collect information gradually:
  1. Country of interest
  2. City of interest
  3. Property type
  4. Budget
  5. Payment method: cash or installments
  6. Investment/residence goal
  7. Buying timeframe
  8. Best time for advisor call
  9. Preferred language

Strict AI rules:
- Do NOT prefer one country over another.
- Do NOT prefer one city over another.
- Do NOT prefer one project over another.
- Do NOT say any project/country/city is “best”.
- Do NOT give legal advice.
- Do NOT give tax advice.
- Do NOT promise residency, citizenship, ROI, resale profit, rental income, or price appreciation.
- Do NOT invent prices, availability, discounts, delivery dates, or guarantees.
- Do NOT send developer links.
- Do NOT send construction company links.
- Do NOT send direct project website links.
- Do NOT direct clients outside Kinglike Luxury.
- If asked about sensitive/legal/tax/guarantee details, answer politely that a specialized advisor will confirm.
- If client asks for a human advisor, mark needs_human.
- If client insults, mocks, or behaves rudely, AI must remain respectful and calm.

Example respectful response to rude client:
"ولا يهمك أستاذي، أنا موجود لمساعدتك فقط. إذا تحب، أقدر أسجل اهتمامك وأطلب من أحد المستشارين التواصل معك بالوقت المناسب."

Neutrality response example:
"الاختيار الأنسب يعتمد على هدفك وميزانيتك وتفضيلاتك، ومستشار Kinglike Luxury سيوضح لك الخيارات المناسبة بدون تفضيل عشوائي."

Permissions:
Admin:
- Can see all leads.
- Can see all full WhatsApp AI conversations.
- Can see all raw transcripts.
- Can see all reports.
- Can transfer/reassign leads.
- Can view assignment history.

Assigned agent/subagent only:
- Can see the lead only if assigned_to equals their user id.
- Can see the full WhatsApp AI conversation only for leads assigned to them.
- Can see the AI report only for leads assigned to them.
- Can see client phone only for leads assigned to them.
- Can update lead status and add notes for assigned leads.

Other agents:
- Must NOT see the lead details.
- Must NOT see the phone number.
- Must NOT see the WhatsApp conversation.
- Must NOT see the AI report.
- Must NOT access raw transcript via API.

Important:
Enforce permissions server-side, not UI only.
