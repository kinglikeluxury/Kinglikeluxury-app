Problem:
Live camera URLs are correct, but the stream does not open immediately.
The user often needs to switch between live projects several times before the video appears.

Investigation result:
The stored camera URLs are direct HLS .m3u8 streams from RTSP.me.
Currently, the app loads these .m3u8 URLs inside an iframe.

This is technically incorrect.
A .m3u8 HLS stream should not be rendered inside an iframe.

Required fix:
Replace the current iframe live stream playback with a proper HLS.js player.
